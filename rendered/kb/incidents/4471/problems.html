The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates come out with day and month swapped: booked 2 March, invited for 3 February. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY under it. People type what the hint says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom looks the same as parser, so intake cannot tell them apart. The parser fix does not touch it: it only happens on the form channel of booking-v3 tenants, and it needs the hint (or the form’s reading) changed.

one-day: the date moves by one day. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting; an invite arrives before the booking. Nothing is swapped. The desk noted these reports by hand (desk-notes.json) and still counted them under parser. They point at time zones, not at date order, and the parser fix will not close them.

How they were counted

  • One report, one id: the ticket export repeats some reports across pages, so duplicates are dropped before counting.
  • By tenant and channel, not by intake’s tag: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser and is wrong often enough that the tag cannot be trusted as a count.
  • Not by subject: the same subject line covers different problems (decision 0001 merges on subject alone; that is how all three ended up in one ticket).
  • Every number on this page is computed from the export when the page renders, so it follows the data as the desk keeps working.

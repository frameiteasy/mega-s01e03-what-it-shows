The two problems

swap: day and month come out swapped. Booked 2 March, invited for 3 February. Every tenant on file writes the day first, and the date is read month first. The kb’s own logic (causes.ts) splits these reports into parser and hint by tenant and channel, but every one of them shows the same thing, and the reporters cannot tell which way in they used. One problem, one ticket: whoever fixes it checks both the shared parser and the booking-v3 form’s MM/DD/YYYY hint.

one-day: the date moves by one day. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting; an invite arrives before the booking. Nothing is swapped. The desk noted these reports by hand (desk-notes.json) and its view keeps them under parser by tenant, so they also count in swap above. They point at time zones, not at date order, and the parser fix will not close them.
